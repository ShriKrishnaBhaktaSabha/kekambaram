The Journey

Milestones from the first Cub Masters' course to the Life Time Achievement Award.

1971
Joined the Movement – Preliminary Course for Cub Masters, July 1971.
1978
Himalaya Wood Badge (Cub Master) – Certificate No. 1611.
1979
First National Jamboree – the 8th, at Maraimalai Nagar, Tamil Nadu.
1992
Assistant Leader Trainer – Certificate No. 1112.
1996
Himalaya Wood Badge (Scout Master) – Certificate No. 5738.
1999
Appointed District Organising Commissioner (Scouts); Life Member of the Bharat Scouts and Guides.
2000
Leader Trainer (Cub) – Certificate No. 349, 24 November 2000.
2005 – 2007
Sub Camp Chief at the 15th National Jamboree (Haridwar) and the Special National Jamboree (Rajarhat).
2016
Sub Camp Chief, 17th National Jamboree, Mysuru; literature-upgradation workshops at NHQ, New Delhi.
2026
Life Time Achievement Award (26 January 2026). Nominated for the NHQ Felicitation and Recognition Ceremony, 4 November 2026.
